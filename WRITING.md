# folkmerdesign.com · Writing rules

Every word on the site follows these rules. Francesca's voice comes first; the source of truth for it is
`../strategia/Strategia 2026-27.md` (tone of voice) and `../linkedin/voce.md` (her voice and the "AI patterns" to remove).

## Voice
- **Competent, concrete, light, honest.** First person ("I"), short sentences, simple verbs.
- Say what happened and what she did. A precise detail beats an adjective.
- **One joke per piece**, at most. Usually self-irony, never at someone else's expense.
- Mix sentence lengths. Most under 20 words; none over 30.

## Never
- The em dash (—). Use a full stop, a colon or a comma.
- Buzzwords: leverage, orchestrate, revolutionise, bridge the gap, seamless, empower, robust, innovative, holistic, game changer, journey (as a metaphor), elevate, unlock (as a metaphor).
- AI patterns (from `voce.md`): "It's not X, it's Y" / "not just X", "Here's why", "The point is", perfectly balanced lists of three, a moral at the end of every paragraph, invented feelings or details.
- Numbers that aren't verified. Always with context. Sky 50% → 85% is measured; Crédit Agricole ~80% is a **target**; MADE ~40% is a **team estimate**.
- Capgemini internal material, or anything Francesca hasn't cleared. Private cases only behind the lock.

## Language and format
- **English (UK)** on the site: personalisation, organised, colour, analyse.
- **Sentence case** for titles and buttons ("Read the case", not "Read The Case"). Eyebrows are uppercase via CSS, written in sentence case in the code.
- **Dates:** "Jul 2025 - Sep 2025" in facts and timelines (3-letter month, spaced hyphen); full months in running text.
- **Numbers:** digits for 10 and up, "+" after ("100+"), thousands with a comma ("12,000").
- **Buttons** start with a verb and say where they go: "Read the case", "Send request", "Back to the homepage".
- **Links** make sense on their own; "(opens in a new tab)" in `sr-only` for external links.
- **Errors** say what happened and what to do, kindly: "That code doesn't work. It may have expired: ask me for a new one."
- **Alt text** on every meaningful image: what it shows and why it matters, in one sentence. Decorative images get `alt=""`.

## Check before publishing
1. Read it out loud. If you wouldn't say it to a colleague, rewrite it.
2. Search for "—", the buzzwords and the AI patterns above.
3. Every number: where does it come from?
4. Every claim about clients: is it cleared?
